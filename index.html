About Daku Fashion

Daku Fashion started with a simple idea: quality shouldn't be reserved for big-budget orders. We source quality fabric from our trusted suppliers, have it produced through our established production partners, and then QC every single piece ourselves before it reaches a customer. The result is a stock of solid-color garments we fully stand behind.

On top of that stock, we offer custom DTF printing — so anyone, from a single customer wanting one personalized tee to a business ordering uniforms, can get exactly the design they want.

Full Custom Production (200 pcs minimum)

For clients with larger requirements, we go beyond printing on our standard stock. From a minimum order of 200 pieces, our team manages complete custom production to your specification — fabric sourcing, trims and components (rib, buttons, cuffs, and more), custom sizing, custom colors, and anything else your production needs. You bring the requirement, we handle the manufacturing.

Export

We ship both retail and bulk custom orders internationally, with completed export work across Asia and Europe — including a full bulk export order of custom printed staff uniforms for Dunkin', the American food & beverage brand, printed with their brand name on the front left chest and a custom design on the back for their employees.

Domestic Delivery

Within Bangladesh, we deliver through Steadfast and Pathao. For bulk orders, if you have a specific courier requirement, we'll do our best to accommodate it.

Privacy Policy

Daku Fashion ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, place an order, or write a review, we collect your name, email address (for enquiries/orders), country, and the order/design/review details you provide, including any image files you upload for printing or for a review.

Reviews

A review you submit (name, star rating, description, and any photo) is sent to us and may be displayed publicly on our Reviews page.

How We Use It

  • To respond to your enquiry and process your order
  • To calculate pricing and prepare your custom print
  • To communicate with you via email or WhatsApp regarding your order

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except as needed to fulfil your order (e.g. courier/shipping partners for delivery).

Data Storage

Enquiry and order details submitted through this site are sent to our order email and are not stored on a public database.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, reach us via the Contact page or WhatsApp.

Terms & Conditions

Orders

Retail orders have no minimum quantity. Wholesale pricing applies to orders of 20 pieces or more. Full custom production (fabric sourcing, trims, custom sizing/color) is available from 200 pieces.

Custom Design & Printing

All custom DTF prints are made to the design, size and placement you specify at order confirmation. Please review your design carefully before confirming — Daku Fashion is not responsible for errors in artwork submitted by the customer.

Pricing

Prices listed are in Bangladeshi Taka (TK) and are subject to change without prior notice. Final price is confirmed at the time of order.

Payment

A 30% advance payment is required to confirm and begin production on every order, paid via bKash, Nagad or Rocket. The remaining balance is settled as agreed at delivery.

Production & Delivery

Production time depends on order size and design complexity and will be communicated at order confirmation. Inside Dhaka delivery is ৳70, outside Dhaka ৳130; bulk order delivery depends on package weight and is confirmed separately. Export/international orders have shipping confirmed per destination.

Returns

As all items are custom printed to order, returns/exchanges are only accepted in case of a printing or production defect.
